# Beatles TYLS Scenario Templates - 2026-06-15

Reusable blank-frame environments for PickArt Beatles/TYLS campaign mockups.

Use these as room/context bases only. Insert product images later using the Phil-marked GIMP aperture-dot workflow so recognisable Beatles faces are preserved from source pixels.

## Templates

1. `beatles_scenario_01_high_end_audio_blank_frame.png`
   - High-end audio listening room.
   - Warm, connoisseur, hi-fi-led.

2. `beatles_scenario_02_bookish_study_cat_blank_frame.png`
   - Bookish collector study with cat attention cue.
   - Cultured, private, literary.

3. `beatles_scenario_03_nordic_simplicity_blank_frame.png`
   - Light Scandinavian simplicity.
   - Pale wood, white walls, restrained and saleable.

4. `beatles_scenario_04_modern_art_collector_blank_frame.png`
   - Modern-art collector apartment/gallery room.
   - Freshest contrast to the listening-room/study family.

5. `beatles_scenario_05_private_bar_dog_blank_frame.png`
   - Private bar / evening dining salon with dog.
   - Intimate and successful; review for darkness/brand-like bottle clutter before campaign approval.

6. `beatles_scenario_06_architectural_hallway_blank_frame.png`
   - Quiet architectural hallway/stair landing.
   - Understated wealth through architecture, light, and materials.

7. `beatles_scenario_07_boho_coffee_shop_brick_window_blank_frame.png`
   - Boho coffee shop by a big window and brick wall.
   - Simpler social-context setting with generic 1960s-style colour advertising in smaller surrounding frames.

8. `beatles_scenario_08_park_easel_beret_blank_frame.png`
   - Framed print on an easel in a park, viewed by an older man in a beret, with two elegant ladies and a small dog in the background.
   - Unusual art-viewing context; use GIMP dots carefully because the easel frame is angled.

9. `beatles_scenario_09_record_bookshop_window_blank_frame.png`
   - Independent record/bookshop window display.
   - Collectible retail discovery setting, with generic 1960s-style colour graphics around the main blank frame.
