## The Beatles Band Image

1. On 15 December 1963, The Beatles taped Thank Your Lucky Stars at Alpha/ATV Studios in Aston, Birmingham; the programme was broadcast on 21 December as Lucky Stars on Merseyside. [S1, S2, S3, S4]
2. Beatles Bible records that several hundred fans waited outside Alpha/ATV Studios in a snowstorm to catch a glimpse of the group. [S1]
3. The Beatles closed the programme with "She Loves You", the song that had already helped turn 1963 into the year of Beatlemania. [S1, S2, S3]
4. The group performed four songs for the programme: "I Want To Hold Your Hand", "All My Loving", "Twist And Shout", and "She Loves You". [S1, S2]
5. Ready Steady Pop notes that The Beatles performed more songs than any other act on that 15 December TYLS special, underlining their headline status. [S2]
6. During the taping, producer George Martin presented the band with gold discs for "She Loves You" and "I Want To Hold Your Hand". [S3, S4]
7. By December 1963, The Beatles had already scored UK Number 1 singles with "From Me To You", "She Loves You", and "I Want To Hold Your Hand". [S7]
8. On the 12 December 1963 Official Singles Chart, "I Want To Hold Your Hand" was at Number 1 and "She Loves You" was still high on the chart, showing the overlap between the two hit records. [S8]
9. The official Beatles site links the release of With The Beatles to the point when the term Beatlemania entered everyday language. [S5]
10. A previous Thank Your Lucky Stars appearance on 20 October 1963 drew such intense fan attention that thousands gathered outside Alpha/ATV Studios, with press reports describing fans trying to storm the studio. [S9]

## John Lennon Image

1. On 15 December 1963, John Lennon was part of a four-song Beatles TYLS set that put the band at the head of the Merseybeat television special. [S1, S2, S3]
2. Lennon's image can be framed against the moment when The Beatles were already dominating Britain but had not yet made their Ed Sullivan breakthrough in America. [S7, S15]
3. "She Loves You" and "I Want To Hold Your Hand" were both Lennon-McCartney songs and both central to the band's late-1963 rise. [S7, S8]
4. With The Beatles, released in November 1963, contained a heavy concentration of Lennon-McCartney material, showing the partnership's speed and importance during the Beatlemania surge. [S5]
5. Lennon had formed The Quarrymen before The Beatles existed, making the 1963 TYLS image part of a rise that began in Liverpool skiffle rather than television polish. [S12]
6. Paul McCartney joined Lennon's Quarrymen in 1957, which makes the December 1963 Lennon image also a snapshot of a songwriting partnership already six years old. [S12]
7. At the Royal Variety Performance on 4 November 1963, Lennon delivered the famous cheap-seats/jewellery line, a televised moment that showed his wit cutting through royal-show formality. [S11]
8. The 15 December TYLS taping came only weeks after the Royal Variety Performance, so Lennon's public persona was sharpening at the same time Beatlemania was exploding. [S11, S1]
9. The October TYLS crowd scenes at Alpha/ATV Studios show the public pressure already gathering around Lennon and the band before the December special. [S9]
10. In late 1963, Lennon was not yet the later solo icon; he was still visibly part of a tight four-man unit being pushed into national obsession. [S1, S2, S5]
