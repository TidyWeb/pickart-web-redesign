The Archive Edition A-100

One hundred prints.
Then the plates are put away.

Every photograph in the PickArt Archive is released as an edition of one hundred prints. No more. Each print is numbered, from 1 to 100, and pressed with the PickArt emboss — the same mark you see here. It is our signature and your proof: ink can be copied, an embossing cannot.

When the hundredth print leaves the gallery, the edition closes — and that image is never printed again. Not for an anniversary. Not for a reissue. Never.

Built to outlast the century

The paper

Platinum Baryta 300 gsm. A heavyweight fibre-based paper with a barium-sulphate coating — the same construction as the silver gelatin papers of the darkroom era. It is the paper museums choose when a photograph is meant to be kept, not replaced.

The mark

Every print is embossed with the PickArt stamp and its edition number, 1 to 100, pressed into the paper itself. Ink can be copied. An embossing cannot.

The certificate

Each print ships with a certificate of authenticity recording the image, the edition and your number within it.
